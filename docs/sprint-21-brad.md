## 1. Serial Killer
| Question | FOUND | Proposal (GUESS) |
|---|---|---|
| Night immunity | ToS2: his attack is "Basic", and a Basic defense stops it. A guide says he has basic defense against attacks | **No immunity.** Immunity is hard to explain, and it makes the Mafia's kill pointless |
| The Doctor saves his victim | Not stated | **Yes**, as "the save beats the kill". The Bodyguard works against him too |
| The Detective's read | ToS2: the Sheriff sees "Innocent" | **"Not Mafia"** |
| He targets a Mafia | Not stated | **Allowed.** The Mafia can kill him as well |
| The last two are him and one Mafia | Sources disagree: some give the Serial Killer the win, some call it a tie, and Town of Salem ends a game no one can win | **A draw** |

**Win:** "kill everyone until only Serial Killers remain" (ToS2). A game with a living Serial Killer is not over unless he has won.

**Fun or not:**
- TheGamer calls him "one of the most fun" roles (FOUND).
- In a small game he dies early or ends it alone (GUESS), so only from 9 players.

## 3. Silencer
- **Variants differ** (FOUND):
  - some silence speech and the vote;
  - some silence speech only;
  - some give the target a vote with no weight;
  - some kill a player who speaks.
- **Proposal (GUESS):**
  - It is announced each morning.
  - He can't pick the same player two nights running.
  - He can pick a teammate, but not himself.
  - The silenced player may still speak and still be voted out.
- Sources:
  - https://bugs.launchpad.net/yandere/+bug/324590
  - https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue5/mafia_roles
