## 1. What others do
1. **Apps sort roles into tiers by player count.**
   - WOLF – Party Game: Classic (4–20), Advanced (6–12, adds Sorcerer, Witch, Hunter), Tactical (9–14), Chaos (10–16, random), Expert (12–20, all roles, Cupid).
   - Source: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6747474675
2. **Ultimate Werewolf's moderator app builds the deck from player count, balance, length and "moderator difficulty"**, and it tells the moderator what to say.
   - Sources: https://beziergames.com/blogs/news/a-new-way-to-run-your-ultimate-werewolf-games, https://apps.apple.com/app/id6446418817
3. **The Hunter and the Mayor are treated as safe extras for any game.**
   - Hunter: takes one player with him on death.
   - Mayor: public, with two votes.
   - Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafia_(party_game), https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue43/roles-of-mafia
4. **Cupid:**
   - He picks the pair on night 1. If one lover dies, the other dies too. The lovers can win as the last two.
   - A Wolf paired with a villager must eliminate everyone. Solo roles keep their own win.
   - Hosts say lovers often don't want Cupid in the game.
   - Sources: Wikipedia, https://help.wolfy.net/en/article/playing-the-cupid-1eo53n8, https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/Tips_werewolves
5. **Town of Salem is where the Veteran, Framer, Lookout (Watcher), Blackmailer and Serial Killer come from.**
   - Sources: https://gamersdecide.com/articles/town-of-salem-roles, https://www.thegamer.com/town-of-salem-best-worst-roles/
6. **The Plaguebearer's transform is real and complex.**
   - The infection spreads hidden. Once every living player is infected, it becomes Pestilence: invincible, and it kills everyone who visits.
   - Source: https://townofsalem.wiki.gg/wiki/Plaguebearer_(ToS2)

## 2. Complaints and wishes
- **Transporter:** in TheGamer's "worst" list ("really hard to pull off correctly"). It was removed from the Town of Salem 2 role list (https://primagames.com/?p=230689).
- **Framer:** "kind of boring and at times seems a bit useless" (TheGamer). It needs a visible payoff.
- **Witch** "incredibly overpowered"; **Serial Killer** "one of the most fun"; **Blackmailer** "a fun, troll role" (TheGamer).
- **Hunter:** hosts add a "silence during the Hunter's shot" rule (BGA tips).
- **Beginners:** the host guide caps them at one or two per table.
- **Pacing:** an odd number of players avoids tied votes. A 1 Mafia vs 1 Town endgame is ruled a Mafia win (Wikipedia).

## 3. Role by role
| Role | Real-game version (FOUND) | Face to face, casual groups (GUESS) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hunter | Shoots one on death, by night or by vote | Dramatic, easy, no night work | Ship first |
| Mayor | Reveals; 2 votes | Simple; becomes a target. With secret votes, double it silently, show totals | Ship first |
| Framer | Framed player reads guilty to the Detective that night | Rewards lying; tell the Mafia the result | Ship first |
| Serial Killer | Kills alone each night | Fun solo; slow if long; dies early in small games | Ship, 9+ |
| Tracker / Watcher | Lookout sees visitors; Tracker sees where the target went | Watcher gives more, easier to explain | Watcher first |
| Cupid / Lovers | Night 1 link; heartbreak; mixed lovers last two | Fun reveals; mixed-lovers is hard for new players | Second wave |
| Silencer | Can't talk next day (online: killed if they talk) | A phone can't hear; enforce "can't vote" | Later, "can't vote" |
| Veteran | Alert up to 3 times; all visitors die | Funny on Mafia, frustrating on the Doctor | Later, chaos only |
| Arsonist | Douse one a night, ignite all | Plain goal, slow, hard to counter | Later |
| Transporter | Swap two players' targets | Rated a worst role; confuses results | Simplify or drop |
| Witch / Puppeteer | Controls one player's action | Overpowered, many exceptions | Drop for now |
| Baker / Plaguebearer | Pestilence transform | Too long and swingy for a party | Drop |

## 5. Mode plans
- **Neutrals:** none under 9 players. 9–12: 1; 13–16: 1–2; 17–20: 2.
- **Mafia:** 2 at 6–8, 3 at 9–12, 4 at 13–16, 5 at 17–20.
- Group the modes by difficulty, starting from an easy starter.

| Mode | Min | Level | 6–8 | 9–12 | 13–16 | 17–20 |
